The

I designed marketing materials for Motorola Solutions, working with marketing stakeholders to understand their goals and turn them into designs that met both creative and strategic objectives.

The work spanned long-form buying guides and reports, product brochures and sales sheets, and digital campaigns, each built inside Motorola's brand system while adapting it to audiences from hospitality to fire and EMS.
