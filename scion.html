Project 01 / 2026

Scion.

Turning a brand's big-picture goals into one immersive visual experience for Scion's Leadership Summit 2026.

Client
Scion, Chicago
Year
2026
Role
Creative direction & design system
Deliverables
Event identity, environmental graphics, wayfinding, merch shop, awards show graphics

The
project

I led creative direction and built the design system for Scion's Leadership Summit 2026, carrying the work from early concept all the way through environmental design and the event's digital touchpoints.

The theme, Momentum: Forward. Faster. Farther., became a visual language of speed lines, condensed type and black and gold, carried across every surface attendees touched, from badges and wayfinding to the stage screens and the ICON Awards night.

01

Momentum identity

The summit theme turned into a logo, a pattern and a palette, then applied to print, badges and registration.

02

Environment & wayfinding

Large-format graphics, screens and signs that carried the theme through the venue and kept attendees moving.

03

Merch shop

A try-it-on, pick-it, order-it shop for Scion apparel, with signage that walked attendees through each step.

04

The Final Percent

A live game show for attendees, with its own title graphic on the main stage screen.

05

ICON Awards 26

The summit's awards night, with its own sub-brand on stage, on screen and on the tables.

Motion

Animated for the stage screen.

An animated logo loop and speaker introductions for the awards night.

Recognition

The night's hardware.

The Full Measure cup and the year's awards, displayed together for the ceremony.
