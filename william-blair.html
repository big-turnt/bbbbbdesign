During the pandemic I worked remotely as a freelance designer for William Blair, collaborating closely with several departments across the firm.

The work below spans employee-spotlight social series, video covers, recruiting and community ads, and internal publications, all built inside the firm's refined serif-and-blue brand.

Our Colleagues series

An Instagram carousel series introducing the people behind the firm: a portrait cover, then their own words.

02

Thinking William Blair

03

Print and digital ads for recruiting and community sponsorships.
