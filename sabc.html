Project 06 / 2020

SABC
Visual ID.

A proposed visual identity system for the Swiss American Business Council, bringing U.S. and Swiss elements together in one mark.

Client
Swiss American Business Council
Year
2020 · 18 weeks
Role
Lead designer
Context
UIC School of Design, mentored by Marcia Lausen

The
brief

How might we combine U.S. and Swiss elements into an interesting, cohesive mark?

Over 18 weeks at the UIC School of Design, I led the design of a new identity for the Swiss American Business Council, moving from research and inspiration through logo studies to a proposed mark and the system around it.

01

Research

The council's existing logo, and a look at how other Swiss business councils present themselves.

02

Inspiration

The cross and the star, and Helvetica: a Swiss typeface made famous in the U.S.

03

Logo studies

Wordmark explorations testing weight, width and contrast across the four letters.

04

Proposed logo

Heavy-to-light Helvetica letters with the Swiss cross stacked over the American star.

05

The system in use

The cross and star become a pattern and a stacked mark across print, signage and merchandise.
